## Look up a gene

Combining the predictions from the six trained networks made it possible to classify
each of the nearly 20,000 human genes analyzed. Thirty-three of them were unanimously
flagged as likely drivers by every network, with no prior evidence classifying them as
such — real candidates for future investigation. On the other end, 1,477 genes were
unanimously classified as passengers.

Some cases stand out for another reason: genes already catalogued as passengers, yet
flagged with high confidence as drivers by every network — like **YWHAZ**, which the
literature has already linked to oncogenic properties in other contexts, even without
being formally catalogued as a cancer driver.
