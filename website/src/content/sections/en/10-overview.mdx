## Overview & access

The needle in the genomic haystack is still there — nearly 20,000 genes, most of
them irrelevant to cancer. What changed is that there's now a map: six protein
interaction networks, multi-omics features, a tuned GCN, and an ensemble that
combines all of it into a single probability per gene, reaching an AUC-PR of 0.677.

This page tells that story in a few minutes of scrolling. The full dissertation has
the 173 pages left out of it: the experiments run on each network in isolation, the
methodological decisions discarded along the way, and the limitations any project
this size carries.

All the code, the training data, and the pipeline that produced the numbers on this
page are also publicly available.
