## A genomic needle in a haystack

Cancer arises from mutations in DNA — but most of them are irrelevant. Out of
thousands of "passenger" mutations, only a handful are "driver" mutations: the
ones that actually push a healthy cell toward cancer.

Finding the genes where these mutations happen — the **cancer driver genes** — is
essential for diagnosis and treatment. But it's like looking for a rare needle in
a haystack of almost 20,000 human genes.

Across the six protein-protein interaction networks used in this dissertation,
genes already confirmed as *drivers* make up less than 10% of the total in any
network — most are labeled *passenger* genes or remain without a confident label
at all. This severe class imbalance is the first major obstacle any model has to
face.
