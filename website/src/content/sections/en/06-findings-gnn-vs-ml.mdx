## GNNs win, GCN leads

We compared the three GNNs with four traditional machine learning algorithms (SVM,
Random Forest, Gradient Boosting Trees, and a simple neural network), all using the
same multi-omics and centrality features. Gradient Boosting Trees (GBT) was a
surprise: it came very close to the GNNs, even beating GAT and GraphSAGE on every
network, and outperforming GCN itself on the MULTINET network.

Even so, looking across this chapter's full set of experiments — different feature
types, different networks, different imbalance strategies — GCN was the model that
most often kept the highest and most consistent performance. That is why it was chosen
for the deep-dive in Chapter 6.
