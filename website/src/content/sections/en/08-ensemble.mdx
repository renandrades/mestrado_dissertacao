## The power of consensus

Each of the six PPI networks, trained in isolation, captures a partial and slightly
different view of the same biology. What if, instead of picking a single network, we
combined the predictions of all of them?

Two ensemble strategies were tested: majority voting across the six networks, and the
average of the probabilities predicted by each. The second — Ensemble-Average —
outperformed not just every individual network, but also a naive "Union" network that
simply merges the nodes and edges of all six into one. The final result: an AUC-PR of
0.677, the best in the dissertation, with a precision of 0.79 while keeping a
competitive recall of 0.46 — the best balance among the approaches tested.
