## Fine-tuning, network by network

With GCN chosen, a new question emerged: what is the best set of hyperparameters for
each PPI network? Up to 864 combinations of architecture, dropout, learning rate, and
*focal loss* configuration were tested for the three smaller networks, and a reduced
grid of 288 combinations for the three larger ones.

After tuning, each model was evaluated on a fixed, independent test set — 1,134 genes
(159 drivers, 975 passengers) common to all six networks, ensuring a fair comparison.
STRING, the largest of the six networks, produced the best individual model, with an
AUC-PR of 0.636.
