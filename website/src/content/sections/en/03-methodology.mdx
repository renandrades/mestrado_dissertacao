## From raw data to a trained model

The methodology unfolds in four major stages. First, **data collection**: six public PPI
networks, four types of multi-omics data (mutations, copy number variation, DNA
methylation and gene expression) covering 16 TCGA cancer types, and gene lists already
confirmed — or ruled out — as cancer drivers.

Next, **pre-processing** standardizes gene identifiers, intersects networks and features so
every node has its own features, and computes centrality measures (degree, betweenness,
closeness, clustering coefficient) for each network.

In the **training** stage, each algorithm is fit and evaluated, class-imbalance mitigation
strategies are tested, and hyperparameters are optimized.

Finally, an **ensemble** approach combines the predictions of models trained on different
PPI networks to reach a more robust prediction.
